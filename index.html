01 FINITE BY DESIGN

You can’t print
more of it.

4,294,967,296. That’s the ceiling.

Nockchain’s consensus rules cap the total issuance of NOCK at 232. New supply follows a defined schedule that eventually ends.

Anyone running a node can check those rules. Scarcity is written into the system.

Explore the monetary policy ↗

02 ANCHORED IN REALITY

Real resources.
Verifiable work.

The digital world needs a physical anchor.

Miners commit energy and hardware to compete for block rewards. Their proof of work makes the chain’s history costly to replace.

Producing the work is hard. Checking the proof is much easier. That asymmetry is the point.
